# Cutting-edge AI in education: products, technologies, AI agents for learning (state as of late Nov 2026)

Method note: research was done with web search only. Fetching was blocked for most primary hosts in this session (openai.com, chalkbeat.org, npr.org, scale.stanford.edu, services.google.com), so many findings come from search snippets of primary pages, or from secondary/aggregator coverage. ## 1. Big-tech education offerings (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft) and national/university deployments

### Takeaway
By late 2026 all four big AI vendors have dedicated "learning modes" that scaffold instead of giving answers: ChatGPT study mode, Claude Learning Mode / Claude for Teachers, Gemini Guided Learning (now default in Google Classroom), Microsoft Copilot "Study and Learn". The fight has moved from universities (2024–25) to K-12 and to whole countries (OpenAI Education for Countries, Estonia AI Leap). There is a visible backlash: districts banning chatbots, unions negotiating binding privacy terms, a conflict over Google AI Overviews in schools.

- "Learning mode as default" is the 2026 battleground: Google making Guided Learning the Classroom default and Microsoft adding a study-only admin mode are both responses to backlash from districts and unions.

## 2. Leading EdTech companies: Khanmigo, Duolingo, Alpha School, Speak, MagicSchool, Brisk, Quizlet, Chegg, Coursera/Udemy

### Takeaway
Duolingo is the clear commercial winner of AI. It has 64.9M DAU (+29%) and an AI Video Call feature whose cost per call fell from about 30 cents to under 1 cent. Chegg is the clear loser, with revenue down about 50% per year. Khanmigo is growing but with weak organic engagement. Alpha School's "2-hour learning" model is scaling fast, with 27 new campuses in fall 2026.

## 3. Agentic and emerging technologies (AI TAs, grading, virtual students, adaptive learning, accessibility, avatars)

### Takeaway
Evidence is converging on one design principle: AI that scaffolds helps, AI that offloads thinking harms (Stanford SCALE, Nov 2026). Products are shifting from chat to agents: Copilot "Study and Learn agent", Claude for Teachers learning state curricula, multi-agent "agentic university" research, NotebookLM's agentic research features. Little 2026-specific evidence was found on virtual students for teacher training, VR+AI, or disability-focused AI.

### Cited Findings
- Stanford SCALE "Evidence Base on AI in K-12: November 2026 Review": AI helps most when it scaffolds learning rather than offloads thinking. College students who learned unfamiliar material with general chatbots performed worse on later assessments taken without AI — [Stanford SCALE](https://scale.stanford.edu/ai/evidence-base-review/evidence-base-ai-k12-november-review) (search snippet)
- "Grade the chat, not just the answer": an emerging assessment practice where teachers evaluate students' AI conversation transcripts — [Google News link](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisAFBVV95cUxNSEpGbmpjR1RPeWFoQ2ZYOWlwVEY4eVdSOGtxZllJd2NLMlRIZGIyWnN0clV0NDRDVVBZNEZfMzI5U0Fncm5jS2NKdy1LZkwwcEE4TzlJRVJtOTdxUEpWQWNnRWFsMkE5S21GWjV1bk5iX2lNTkYxX0p3bzBkcGw2OTl5cUNCZWREUkt4c3dYS2t2eHlOMUJkbThsR2p5T1JSRGtDQ3ZMNEczc1RQczYzWQ?oc=5&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US%3Aen) (headline only)
- Multi-agent research: "Agentic University: Collaborative Agents for Personalized Learning" (arXiv, Oct 2026) — [arXiv 2610.40492](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40492)
- Agentic curriculum features: Claude for Teachers is described as "agentic AI learns your state's curriculum" — [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320567/20260715/claude-now-free-us-teachers-agentic-ai-learns-your-states-curriculum.htm). Copilot Teach generates multi-week Unit Plans — [WindowsForum](https://windowsforum.com/news/microsoft-365-copilot-teach-rolls-out-multi-week-unit-plans-for-educators.450291/)
- AI grading: CoGrader, Gradescope and others offer LMS-integrated feedback — [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/10-best-ai-tools-for-education/). Russia's Yandex Uchebnik is piloting automatic essay checking (see section 5)
- Georgia Tech's Jill Watson (AI TA, since 2016; ChatGPT-based version 2023–24) remains the canonical AI TA case [background, URL not re-verified]
- Policy guardrails: Katy ISD (Texas) banned generative AI chatbots for grades K-6, with scaffolded access through grade 12 — [aiagentstore roundup](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/topic/education-learning)

### Inferences
- The 2026 "agentic" frontier in education is mainly teacher-side automation (unit planning, grading, curriculum alignment) and mode control (study-only tenants). Fully autonomous student tutors remain contested.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2026 sources found on virtual/simulated students for teacher training, VR/AR+AI, knowledge tracing deployments, AI avatars or disability-specific AI. The report writer should flag these as under-documented in this research.

## 4. Market size, funding and usage statistics

### Takeaway
VC funding for EdTech is still depressed: about $2.4–2.6B in 2025, and about $1B in H1 2026 (−26% YoY), concentrated in AI and workforce platforms. Market-size estimates for "AI in education" range from about $8B (2025) to $32B (2030). Adoption is near-universal among UK undergraduates (about 95%), and over half of US teens and teachers use AI.

### Cited Findings
**Funding / market**
- HolonIQ: $2.6B EdTech VC in 2025 (about +11% vs 2024), "bigger bets in AI and workforce training" — [HolonIQ](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/edtech-hits-2-6b-in-investment-as-the-market-stabilizes-bigger-bets-in-ai-and-workforce-training). HolonIQ's 2026 Outlook says $2.4B — [HolonIQ](https://holoniq.com/notes/2026-global-education-outlook) (internal inconsistency)
- Q1 2026: $512M across 63 deals, a "slow start", with capital favoring AI-enabled, career-aligned platforms — [HolonIQ](https://www.holoniq.com/notes/512m-in-q1-signals-a-slow-start-to-2026-with-capital-continuing-to-favor-ai-enabled-career-aligned-platforms). H1 2026 about $1B, −26% YoY (secondary summary of HolonIQ) — [SearchLab](https://searchlab.nl/en/statistics/education-edtech-statistics-2026)
- Deal count up 11% while median round size fell 31% — [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/education-technology/edtech-deals-rose-11-while-the-median-funding-round-shrank-31)
- AI-in-education market: about $8.3B (2025) rising to $32.3B (2030) — [Hyde Park Capital Q2 2026](https://www.hydeparkcapital.com/insights/industry-reports/educational-technology-market-insights-q2-2026/). Total EdTech market about $213–238B in 2026 (Grand View / Business Research Co.) — [Hyde Park](https://www.hydeparkcapital.com/insights/industry-reports/educational-technology-market-insights-q2-2026/)
- Claimed $4.2B of VC into EdTech AI in 2025 — [valueaddvc](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/ai-tutors-are-here-how-duolingo-khan-academy-and-startups-are-personalizing-education) (unverified; conflicts with HolonIQ's total)

**Usage surveys**
- Pew (Feb 24, 2026; n=1,458 US teens aged 13–17; fieldwork Sep 2025):
  - 54% have used chatbots for schoolwork help, and 57% to search for information
  - 10% do all or most of their schoolwork with chatbots; 21% some; 23% a little
  - All or most: 20% in households earning under $30K, versus 7% in households earning $75K+
  - 59% think AI cheating is regular at their school
  - [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/02/24/how-teens-use-and-view-ai/)
- HEPI Student Generative AI Survey 2026 (UK; published Mar 12, 2026; n=1,054): about 95% use AI and about 94% use GenAI for assessed work. 12% put AI-generated text directly into assessed work (8% in 2025, 3% in 2024) — [whizi summary](https://whizi.io/resources/ai-in-education-statistics/) (secondary)
- Gallup–Walton: 60% of US public K-12 teachers used AI in 2024-25. A Gallup release of Oct 14, 2026 reportedly gives 59% for 2025-26. Only about 18% received formal guidance — [theagentalmanac](https://theagentalmanac.com/articles/how-real-teachers-use-ai-2026); [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/ai-in-education-statistics/) (secondary, conflicting)
- RAND: 53% of US ELA, math and science teachers used AI for planning or teaching in spring 2025, up from 25% a year earlier. 62% of 12–29-year-olds used AI for homework (Dec 2025 panel) — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/ai-in-education-statistics/) (secondary)
- UK teachers: 80% use AI (Bett UK poll, Jun 2026) — [whizi](https://whizi.io/resources/ai-in-education-statistics/) (secondary)
- Digital Education Council Global AI Student Survey 2024: 86% of students use AI, 54% weekly [background, URL not re-verified]. No 2026 edition found

### Inferences
- Adoption has saturated faster than funding recovered. Money flows to AI features inside incumbents (Duolingo, Google, Microsoft) rather than to new EdTech startups.

### Gaps
- Primary Gallup Oct 2026 and HEPI 2026 reports were not opened directly. A Digital Education Council 2026 survey was not found.

## 5. Russia and CIS context

### Takeaway
Russia is building a "sovereign" school AI. In Nov 2026 the Ministry of Education and Sber agreed to build a GigaChat-based model for schools. Russia also has a first AI textbook for grades 5–9 (Feb 2026), new AI sections in state informatics textbooks developed with Yandex, and ministry guidelines on neural networks in schools (Sep 2026). Kazakhstan is in OpenAI's Education for Countries cohort.

### Cited Findings
- Nov 2026: the Ministry of Education (Минпросвещения) and Sber signed an agreement to jointly develop a sovereign AI model for school education on the GigaChat base. Minister Kravtsov said it complements rather than replaces teachers. A presentation is expected before the end of the school year — [1sept.ru, 15.11.2026](https://1sept.ru/news/2026/11/15/74491); [Mail.ru News](https://news.mail.ru/society/73106256/); [Vaibix](https://www.vaibix.ru/blog/ai-news/suverennaya-neyset-dlya-shkol-gigasber)
- Feb 2026: "Introduction to Artificial Intelligence" (grades 5–9, by the AI Alliance and Prosveshchenie) was added to the federal list of textbooks. Students build projects on GigaChat — [Учительская газета (vogazeta)](https://edition.vogazeta.ru/articles/2026/2/19/schoolbook/27064-minprosvescheniya_vklyuchilo_v_shkolnuyu_programmu_pervyy_uchebnik_po_ii)
- AI sections will go into new state informatics textbooks developed by the Russian Academy of Sciences (RAN) with Yandex. Kravtsov said AI should act as a mentor that leads to the answer, not one that gives it — [Profile.ru](https://profile.ru/news/society/shkolniki-budut-izuchat-iskusstvennyj-intellekt-v-kurse-informatiki-1925906/)
- Sep 2026: the Ministry's methodological recommendations regulated neural-network use in general-education schools for the first time. Yandex Uchebnik is piloting automatic essay checking and an AI course for teachers. Pilots at Letovo, Khoroshkola and Uchi.ru (Tatarstan, Novosibirsk) remain localized — [InternetUrok blog](https://interneturok.ru/blog/uchebnye_voprosy/ii-v-obrazovanii-novye-pravila)
- A ministry letter of Aug 31, 2026 reportedly allows teachers to use AI at all stages, with 250K teachers to be trained in AI by year-end — [Habr](https://habr.com/ru/articles/1100082/) (low reliability, unconfirmed)
- Kazakhstan: in OpenAI's first Education for Countries cohort (Jan 2026) — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/the-next-phase-of-education-for-countries/)

### Inferences
- The Russian model is state-led and vendor-consolidated (Sber for the model, Yandex for textbooks and platform). It uses the same "mentor, not answer machine" framing as the Western study modes.
