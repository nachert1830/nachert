## 1. International and national policy frameworks (newest first)

### Takeaway
In 2025–2026 policy split two ways. Some actors **mandated AI education**: UAE, China, the US executive order, Estonia, and Russia (step by step). Others **regulated or rolled back AI in classrooms**: South Korea's AIDT reversal, NYC's moratorium for grades up to 8 (Sept 2026), and the EU AI Act's high-risk regime, which has been delayed to Dec 2027. International bodies (UNESCO, OECD, EC) now concentrate on AI literacy frameworks and on evidence that only pedagogically designed AI helps learning.

- Russia is following a gradual, informatics-led and largely optional path rather than a UAE-style mandate.

## 2. Academic integrity, AI detectors and assessment shifts

### Takeaway
Student use of GenAI for coursework is close to universal (UK: 88% in 2025). Proven AI-misconduct cases are rising quickly but represent "the tip of the iceberg". Detectors are unreliable and biased, so institutions are moving to in-class, handwritten and oral assessment.

### Cited Findings
- 2026: Stanford AI Index 2026 coverage reports that 4 of 5 US high-school and college students use AI for schoolwork. Only about half of middle and high schools have any AI policy, and only 6% of teachers say their school's AI policy is clear. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/stanford-ai-index-2026-education)
- Jun 2025, Guardian FOI investigation (UK):
  - About 7,000 proven AI-cheating cases in 2023-24, or 5.1 per 1,000 students, up from 1.6.
  - Projected to reach about 7.5 per 1,000 in 2024-25.
  - Traditional plagiarism fell from 19 to 15.2 per 1,000.
  - More than 27% of universities did not record AI misuse as a separate category.
  - Sources: [The Register, 16 Jun 2025](https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/16/university_ai_cheating/); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/uk-universities-ai-cheating-guardian/); [IET](https://engx.theiet.org/b/articles/posts/7-000-cases-of-ai-cheating-among-university-students-just-the-tip-of-the-iceberg)
- Feb 2025, HEPI survey: 88% of UK students use GenAI for assessments, up from 53% in 2024. — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/uk-universities-ai-cheating-guardian/)
- University of Reading study: AI-written exam submissions went undetected 94% of the time. — [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/uk-universities-ai-cheating-guardian/)
- Return to paper: UC Berkeley's store saw an 80% jump in blue-book sales over two academic years as professors returned to handwritten, in-class essays. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/16/university_ai_cheating/)
- OECD/TALIS 2024: 72% of lower-secondary teachers think AI can harm academic integrity. — [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html)
- **Detector bias.** Liang et al., "GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers", *Patterns*, 10 July 2023.
  - Seven popular detectors had an **average false-positive rate of 61.2% on 91 TOEFL essays** by Chinese students, versus near-zero on 88 US 8th-grade essays.
  - The cause is lower perplexity from more limited vocabulary.
  - Simple prompting both reduced the bias and bypassed the detectors.
  - The authors caution against using detectors in evaluative or educational settings.
  - Sources: [arXiv 2304.02819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819); [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10382961/)
- A 2026 follow-up preprint revisits the bias against non-native speakers in newer detectors. — [arXiv 2602.05769](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.05769) (findings not reviewed in detail)

### Inferences
- Detection-based enforcement is weak on both counts: it misses most AI use (94% undetected at Reading) and falsely accuses some groups (61% FPR for non-native writers). This drives "assessment redesign": oral vivas, supervised in-class work, process portfolios, and "two-lane" policies that permit AI in some tasks and ban it in secured ones.

### Gaps
- No primary 2026 data was found on Turnitin AI-detection volumes or on universities disabling detectors (e.g., Vanderbilt and others turned off Turnitin AI detection in 2023 [background]).
- No systematic data was found on the spread of oral exams.

## 3. Risks: privacy, bias, equity, teen mental health and AI companions, deskilling, over-reliance

### Takeaway
The sharpest new risk area in 2025–26 is **AI companions and teen mental health**. It prompted the first US state law (California SB 243), an FTC 6(b) inquiry, and NYC's all-grades ban on companion bots. Equity, privacy and over-reliance (the "exam reversal" effect) remain central to OECD, UNESCO and China's guidance.

### Cited Findings
- **AI companions (Common Sense Media, July 2025).** In a survey of 1,060 US teens aged 13–17 (Apr–May 2025), **72% had used AI companions** and 52% were regular users. About a third were as satisfied talking to AI as to friends. Half distrust the advice, and 80% still spend more time with real friends. CSM recommends **no one under 18** use AI companions. — [Common Sense Media](https://commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/nearly-3-in-4-teens-have-used-ai-companions-new-national-survey-finds); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/21/72-of-u-s-teens-have-used-ai-companions-study-finds/)
- **FTC, Sept 2025.** 6(b) orders went to 7 firms: Alphabet, Character Technologies, Instagram, Meta, OpenAI, Snap and xAI. They cover how the firms test companion-bot safety, limit harms to children, monetise engagement and handle conversation data. — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-launches-inquiry-ai-chatbots-acting-companions)
- **California SB 243.** Signed 13 Oct 2025 and effective 1 Jan 2026, it is the first US companion-chatbot safety law. It requires disclosure and suicide/self-harm protocols. Annual reporting to the Office of Suicide Prevention starts 1 July 2027. It provides a private right of action of at least $1,000 per violation. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/13/california-becomes-first-state-to-regulate-ai-companion-chatbots); [Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2025/10/new-california-companion-chatbot-law); [FPF](https://fpf.org/blog/understanding-the-new-wave-of-chatbot-legislation-california-sb-243-and-beyond/)
- NYC (Sept 2026) bans companion chatbots in all grades and sets screen-time caps. — [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/new-york-city-pauses-ai-use-for-elementary-middle-school-students/829496/)
- EU AI Omnibus adds a new prohibited practice on AI that generates non-consensual intimate content or CSAM (applies from about Dec 2026, per law-firm summaries). — [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/07/EU-AI-Act-Update-Digital-Omnibus-Finalizes-8-Compliance-Changes)
- **Parents (National Parents Union, survey Sept 2026, reported 3 Nov 2026).** 77% want schools to teach effective AI use, and **71% support banning student chatbot use during the school day**. — [EdSurge](https://edsurge.com/news/survey-most-parents-want-students-to-use-ai-in-school-with-strong-safeguards)
- **Over-reliance.** OECD DEO 2026: performance gains from general GenAI vanish or reverse when it is removed in exams. — [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html). Russia's minister warned that fully delegating tasks to neural nets does not build knowledge. — [Минпросвещения](https://xn--90aivcdt6dxbc.xn--p1ai/articles/news/s-2027-goda-uroki-matematiki-biologii-khimii-i-fiziki-stanut-bolee-prikladnymi/)
- **Teacher deskilling and replacement.** China's 2025 guidance forbids teachers from handing their core teaching role to AI. — [China Daily](https://govt.chinadaily.com.cn/s/202505/16/WS6826de33498eec7e1f736de6/guideline-to-regulate-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-schools.html)
- **Privacy and surveillance.** The EU bans emotion recognition in schools (from 2 Feb 2025). Exam-proctoring and behaviour-monitoring AI is high-risk under Annex III. — [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj). Privacy International lists Korea's AIDT among data-protection concerns. — [Privacy International](https://privacyinternational.org/examples/5708/ai-powered-textbooks-fail-make-grade-south-korea)
- **Equity.** OECD calls for equitable infrastructure. — [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html). EdWeek (June 2026) notes that students experience AI in very different ways, with uneven access. — [EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/technology/students-are-experiencing-ai-in-very-different-ways-is-that-a-problem/2026/06). Detector bias against non-native writers is itself an equity harm. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02819)

### Inferences
- Policy attention has moved from cheating (2023) to child safety and wellbeing (2025–26). Companion bots are now treated as a distinct, higher-risk category than learning tools: NYC bans them at every grade while allowing vetted high-school pilots.

### Gaps
- The status of the FTC inquiry (any report or enforcement as of Nov 2026) was not found.

## 4. Teachers: usage, time savings, training and workforce

### Takeaway
Teacher AI use is spreading but uneven. Frequent users report saving about 6 hours a week. Most teachers lack training and clear policy. Industry-funded training at scale (the AFT academy) is the main US response and is debated as corporate influence.

### Cited Findings
- **Gallup–Walton Family Foundation (released 25 June 2025).**
  - Sample: 2,232 US public K-12 teachers, surveyed 18 Mar–11 Apr 2025.
  - 60% used AI in 2024-25: 32% weekly, 28% infrequently, 40% not at all.
  - Weekly users save **5.9 h/week**, about **6 weeks per school year** (monthly users: 2.9 h).
  - Only 19% work at schools with an AI policy. Schools with a policy show a 26% larger "AI dividend".
  - 68% received no AI training.
  - Time savings are self-reported.
  - Sources: [Walton FF](https://www.waltonfamilyfoundation.org/the-ai-dividend-new-survey-shows-ai-is-helping-teachers-reclaim-valuable-time); [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/survey-60-of-teachers-used-ai-this-year-and-saved-up-to-6-hours-of-work-a-week/); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/06/25/teachers-ai-tools-grading-lessons-say-it-saves-them-6-hours-a-week/); [Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/poll/691967/teachers-using-ai-tools-save-hours-week.aspx)
- **Gallup–Walton 2026 follow-up (via search summary).** 48% of teachers received only informal AI guidance, and 34% received none. — [Gallup 710534](https://news.gallup.com/poll/710534/teachers-receive-no-formal-guidance.aspx) (page not fetched, host blocked)
- June 2026: most US K-12 teachers say AI's impact on education will exceed that of the internet or computers. — [Michigan Public/NPR](https://www.michiganpublic.org/2026-06-05/most-k-12-teachers-say-ais-impact-on-education-will-eclipse-the-internet-or-computers)
- May 2026, EdWeek: teachers call the lack of AI guidance a major problem. E4E's "Voices from the Classroom" found fewer than a quarter of teachers report adequate training to support student AI use. — [EdWeek](https://www.edweek.org/technology/teachers-say-lack-of-ai-guidance-is-a-major-problem/2026/05); [EdSurge](https://edsurge.com/news/survey-most-parents-want-students-to-use-ai-in-school-with-strong-safeguards)
- International: England (NEU State of Education 2026): about 66% of teachers in both 2025 and 2026 reported no school policy on student AI use. — [NEU](https://neu.org.uk/latest/press-releases/state-education-2026-ai). ### Inferences
- The "AI dividend" is real but concentrated among frequent users and in schools that have policies. The binding constraints are policy clarity and training, not access to tools.
- Workforce framing is currently augmentation (reclaiming time). China's rule against replacing teachers shows replacement fears are on the agenda.

### Gaps

## 5. Future of work and skills: what should be taught

### Takeaway
Employers expect 39% of core skills to change by 2030, with AI the most transformative force. Curricula are responding with AI literacy (UNESCO, EC–OECD, PISA 2029 MAIL) and more applied and critical-thinking content, not just coding.

### Cited Findings
- **WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 (Jan 2025).**
  - 170M jobs created and 92M displaced by 2030, a net +78M (created jobs equal 14% of current employment).
  - **39% of key skills will change by 2030**, down from 44% in the 2023 report.
  - 86% of employers expect AI and information processing to transform their business.
  - Survey of 1,000+ employers covering 14M+ workers in 55 economies.
  - Sources: [WEF press](https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-78-million-new-job-opportunities-by-2030-but-urgent-upskilling-needed-to-prepare-workforces/); [WEF story](https://weforum.org/stories/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-jobs-of-the-future-and-the-skills-you-need-to-get-them)
- WEF 2025 fastest-growing skills: AI and big data, networks and cybersecurity, and tech literacy, plus creative thinking, resilience, flexibility and agility, and curiosity and lifelong learning [background; consistent with the WEF report]. — [WEF story](https://weforum.org/stories/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-jobs-of-the-future-and-the-skills-you-need-to-get-them)
- PISA 2029 MAIL will for the first time measure students' media and AI literacy internationally. — [AI Literacy Framework](https://ailiteracyframework.org/blog/shaping-future-success-ai-literacy-framework-pisa-2029-mail/)
- Curriculum shifts:
  - UAE strands include ethics and policy. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/education/uae-to-launch-ai-curriculum-starting-in-kindergarten-7-core-concepts-revealed-1.500115415)
  - Russia emphasises using ready-made AI tools over coding from scratch, and more applied STEM from 2027. — [Interfax](https://interfax.ru/russia/1107885)
  - China uses a tiered spiral AI-literacy curriculum. — [Australian DoE brief](https://www.education.gov.au/vi/node/19495)
  - The US EO pushes AI apprenticeships. — [Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-04-28/html/2025-07368.htm)

### Inferences
- There is broad convergence that "what to teach" means AI literacy (concepts, critical evaluation, ethics, responsible use) together with durable human skills. The exam-reversal evidence argues for keeping core-skill practice AI-free while teaching AI use explicitly.

### Gaps
- The WEF Future of Jobs 2026 or 2027 edition (if any) was not checked.
- There is no hard evidence yet linking AI-literacy curricula to labour-market outcomes.
